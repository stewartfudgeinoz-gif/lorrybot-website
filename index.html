Frequently asked questions

Will this sound like a robot answering my phone?+

No — it's built to handle a real booking conversation properly, not read out a script. It's designed to capture the details a booking actually needs, the way a competent person taking the call would.

How much does this cost?+

Get in touch for a free discovery call and we'll talk through pricing based on your business — as a founding client, you'll also get founding-client terms, including no lock-in for your first month.

How long until this is up and running?+

The aim is to have you live within 1–2 weeks of our first conversation. There's no lengthy IT project — you don't need to learn new software.

Does it work for local runs and interstate runs?+

Yes. Whether your freight is going local, like Sydney to Sydney, or interstate, like Sydney to Melbourne, LorryBot captures the same booking details — pickup and delivery addresses, contact details, freight type and timing — and logs them into your existing system.

Do I need to replace my current system?+

No. LorryBot logs bookings into whatever you already use — a spreadsheet, a docket process, or a TMS if you've got one. This isn't a rip-and-replace platform.

What happens to the booking details once they're captured?+

They're logged into your existing system in a structured, consistent format — the same details every time, rather than rushed handwritten notes.

Is my data secure?+

Yes — booking and customer details are handled with the same care as any business system you'd trust with customer information, and nothing is shared outside of what's needed to run your bookings properly.

What if I already have a TMS?+

LorryBot can log bookings directly into most existing systems. We'll confirm exactly how that works for your setup on the discovery call.

Do I need to be technical to use this?+

No. It's built to run in the background — you don't need to configure or manage anything day-to-day. That's the point.
